# Educational Assessment & Examinations Services (EAES)
## National Assessment Protocols, High-Stakes Examinations & Integrity Frameworks

### 1. Executive Mandate & Institutional Governance
The **Educational Assessment and Examinations Service (EAES)** (Amharic: የትምህርት ምዘናና ፈተናዎች አገልግሎት) is the autonomous federal agency operating under the supervisory umbrella of the FDRE Ministry of Education responsible for designing, validating, securing, printing, administering, and grading all national standardized educational assessments in Ethiopia.

The agency's primary statutory objectives are:
- Eliminating exam malpractice, security breaches, and test item leakages.
- Standardizing psychometric evaluation across all regions and linguistic groups.
- Establishing transparent, meritocratic benchmarks for tertiary education admissions.
- Providing empirical diagnostic data to the Ministry of Education on national learning achievement trends.

---

### 2. The Three-Tier National Examination Architecture

#### A. Grade 6: Primary School Completion Assessment
- **Administrative Jurisdiction**: Administered regionally under the direction of Regional Education Bureaus (REBs) with national test design guidelines issued by the MOE.
- **Objective**: Evaluates mastery of foundational literacy, numeracy, and environmental science competencies; acts as the gateway for transition into Middle School (Grade 7).
- **Core Subject Battery**: Mother Tongue Language, Federal Working Language (Amharic), English, Mathematics, Environmental Science, and Moral/Citizenship Education.

#### B. Grade 8: Middle School Leaving Examination (Regional Standardized)
- **Administrative Jurisdiction**: Executed uniformly by Regional Education Bureaus across all school clusters.
- **Objective**: Serves as a high-stakes certification exam determining progression into General Secondary School (Grade 9).
- **Core Subject Battery**: English, Mathematics, General Science, Social Studies, Career and Technical Education (CTE), Information Technology, and Citizenship Education.
- **Grading & Certification**: Regional pass-mark thresholds determine admission into government secondary schools versus alternative technical pathways.

#### C. Grade 12: Ethiopian Secondary School Leaving Certificate Examination (ESSLCE)
- **Administrative Jurisdiction**: Solely owned, designed, and executed by the Federal EAES.
- **Target Population**: Approximately 700,000 to 1,000,000 Grade 12 candidates annually across public and private secondary schools.
- **Function**: The paramount national determinant for award of the high school completion certificate, placement into public higher education institutions, and eligibility for vocational polytechnic diploma programs.

---

### 3. Subject Clusters for Grade 12 ESSLCE
Candidates sit for exams based on their Senior Secondary track:

| Stream | Examination Battery (Total 600 / 700 Marks) | Scoring Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Natural Science** | English, Mathematics (Natural), Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Civics / Scholastic Aptitude | 50% (300/600 or 350/700) for standard direct university entry |
| **Social Science** | English, Mathematics (Social), Geography, History, Economics, Civics / Scholastic Aptitude | 50% (300/600 or 350/700) for standard direct university entry |

*Note: Special affirmative action cutoffs are officially established annually for female students, emerging pastoralist regions (Afar, Somali, Benishangul-Gumuz, Gambella), and candidates with recognized physical and visual disabilities.*

---

### 4. University-Based Exam Administration & Integrity Reforms (2022–Present)
To dismantle systemic examination leakage, local bribery, and digital cheating rings that historically plagued neighborhood secondary testing centers, the Ministry instituted an unprecedented operational reform:
1. **Relocation to Public University Campuses**: All Grade 12 candidates are transported from their home woredas to live inside secured public university dormitories for the duration of the examination week.
2. **Security & Signal Demarcation**: Campuses operate under strict perimeter security with localized mobile network jamming, biometric verification of candidates, and prohibition of all unauthorized digital hardware.
3. **Transition to Computer-Based Testing (CBT)**: Systematic rollout of digital exam portals hosted on localized campus offline servers, providing randomized question sequences and instant encrypted submission.
4. **Independent Invigilation**: Invigilation is executed by university academic staff and federal security supervisors rather than local high school teachers.

---

### 5. The National Remedial Program Framework
Following the enforcement of strict exam integrity in 2022/2023, where only a disciplined minority of candidates scored above the 50% cutoff, the Ministry designed the **National Remedial Program**:
- **Target Group**: Students who scored within a defined margin below the direct university cutoff (typically 30% to 49%).
- **Delivery**: A rigorous 4-to-6 month intensive boarding curriculum hosted on public university campuses focusing on foundational English, Mathematics, Physics/Chemistry (for Natural Science), and Economics/Geography (for Social Science).
- **Exit Assessment**: Candidates sit for a centralized National Remedial Exit Exam. Successful candidates qualify for freshman undergraduate admission in public universities.

---

### 6. National Higher Education Exit Examinations
Introduced comprehensively in the 2022/2023 academic year:
- **Mandate**: Mandatory for all graduating undergraduate seniors in both public and private higher education institutions across Ethiopia.
- **Objective**: Standardize degree credibility, verify graduate competency against national labor market needs, and prevent degree mill inflation in unaccredited programs.
- **Modalities**: Administered electronically through centralized university digital centers, covering core disciplinary competency domains.
