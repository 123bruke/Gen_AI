# FDRE Ministry of Education: Teachers & Educational Leaders Development Policy
## National Competency Standards, Pre-Service Preparation, CPD & Licensing Frameworks

### 1. Strategic Imperative
The **Teachers and Educational Leaders Development Executive** of the FDRE Ministry of Education coordinates policies and quality standards governing the recruitment, preparation, continuous professional enhancement, certification, and career progression of all pedagogical personnel across Ethiopia's general education sector.

Recognizing that educational quality cannot exceed the professional quality and moral commitment of classroom educators, the framework establishes rigorous national standards to transform teaching into a prestigious, licensed, and highly regulated profession.

---

### 2. Pre-Service Teacher Preparation Models

#### A. Primary & Middle School Teachers: Colleges of Teacher Education (CTEs)
- **Institutional Context**: Over 36 regional Colleges of Teacher Education (CTEs) across all regions prepare primary educators.
- **Entry Qualifications**: Candidates must successfully complete Grade 12 with competitive ESSLCE scores and demonstrate language proficiency in the designated regional mother tongue.
- **Curricular Model**: 3-year and 4-year Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.) programs integrating child development psychology, mother-tongue linguistics, instructional design, assessment theory, and a mandatory 6-month school practicum.

#### B. Secondary School Teachers: Postgraduate Diploma in Teaching (PGDT)
- **Model**: Concurrent and post-degree professional teacher preparation.
- **Prerequisite**: A Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) or Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) degree in a specialized academic discipline (e.g., Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, History, Geography, English) from an accredited university.
- **Training Duration**: 1-year full-time or 2-summer intensive professional diploma program administered by university faculties of education covering pedagogy, adolescent psychology, curriculum adaptation, and classroom action research.

---

### 3. National Teacher Licensing & Competency Certification Framework
Under the General Education Proclamation No. 1368/2025, possession of an academic degree or diploma does not automatically grant the legal right to teach in Ethiopian classrooms. Every practicing educator must attain a **National Professional Teaching License**:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                    NATIONAL TEACHER LICENSING WORKFLOW                          |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. ACADEMIC QUALIFICATION : Verified B.Ed. or B.Sc./B.A. + PGDT                 |
|                                     v                                           |
| 2. NATIONAL LICENSING EXAM : Centralized Assessment on:                         |
|    * Subject Matter Mastery (50%)                                               |
|    * Pedagogical Knowledge & Learning Sciences (30%)                            |
|    * Professional Ethics, Inclusiveness & Legal Responsibilities (20%)          |
|                                     v                                           |
| 3. CLASSROOM PRACTICUM AUDIT : Verified school-level teaching demonstration     |
|                                     v                                           |
| 4. ISSUANCE OF LICENSE : Valid for 3 to 5 years subject to CPD renewal credits |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### 4. Continuous Professional Development (CPD) & License Renewal
To maintain an active teaching license, teachers must complete accredited **Continuous Professional Development (CPD)** cycles:
- **Mandatory CPD Hours**: Minimum of 60 verified CPD hours per academic year.
- **Core Priority Themes**:
  1. Differentiated Instruction for Mixed-Ability Classrooms.
  2. Integrating ICT, E-Learning Portals and Digital STEM tools.
  3. Formative Assessment & Diagnostic Feedback Techniques.
  4. Gender-Sensitive Pedagogy and Special Needs Inclusive Support.
  5. Action Research on Classroom Learning Challenges.
- **Portfolio Assessment**: Teachers maintain an active professional portfolio containing lesson plans, student progress records, peer observation notes, and reflective essays.

---

### 5. National Teacher Career Progression Ladder
The Ministry has established a standardized five-tier professional career structure:

| Career Tier | Minimum Experience | Qualification & Licensing Requirement | Professional Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beginner Teacher** | 0 – 2 Years | Valid Initial License, B.Ed. or PGDT | Supervised instruction, induction mentoring participation. |
| **Junior Teacher** | 3 – 5 Years | Standard License, Satisfactory CPD Portfolio | Independent classroom instruction, student club leadership. |
| **Senior Teacher** | 6 – 10 Years | Senior License, Superior Evaluation, Action Research | Department head, peer mentoring of beginner educators. |
| **Lead Teacher** | 11 – 15 Years | Advanced License, Regional Training Certification | School-wide pedagogical coach, curriculum evaluator. |
| **Master Teacher** | 16+ Years | Master Teacher License, Published Educational Innovations | Zonal/Regional curriculum advisor, CTE adjunct lecturer. |

---

### 6. Educational Leadership & School Principal Certification
The policy recognizes school principals and woreda supervisors not as mere bureaucrats, but as **Instructional Leaders**:
- Requirement of a Master’s Degree in Educational Planning and Management (EDPM) or Educational Leadership.
- Mandatory School Leadership Licensing Examination evaluating institutional budgeting, instructional supervision, community mobilization, and crisis management.
