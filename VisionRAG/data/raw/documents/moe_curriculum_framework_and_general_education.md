# FDRE Ministry of Education: National General Education Curriculum Framework
## Comprehensive Structure, Learning Pathways & Subject Architecture (KG to Grade 12)

### 1. The Pedagogical Reform & Paradigm Shift
The FDRE Ministry of Education initiated a comprehensive structural and pedagogical overhaul of the national general education system, replacing the legacy 8-4 structure (8 years of primary and 4 years of secondary) with the modern **6-2-4 structural model**:
- **Primary Education (Grades 1–6)**: Foundational and intermediate literacy, numeracy, citizenship, and environmental sciences.
- **Middle School Education (Grades 7–8)**: Transitional junior secondary level introducing subject specialization, laboratory sciences, and pre-vocational skills.
- **Secondary Education (Grades 9–12)**: Divided into General Secondary (Grades 9–10) and Senior Secondary Specialized Streams (Grades 11–12: Natural Science vs Social Science).

The framework emphasizes competency-based learning (CBL), inquiry-based problem solving, indigenous knowledge systems, digital competency, and character formation.

### 3. Subject Matrix by Educational Tier

#### Tier 1: Primary Education (Grades 1–6)
Primary education establishes foundational cognitive and behavioral skills using the learner's home language (Mother Tongue):
1. **Mother Tongue Language Arts**: Reading, creative writing, and oral rhetoric in designated regional languages (e.g., Afaan Oromoo, Amharic, Tigrinya, Somali, Sidama, Wolaytta, Hadiyya, Afar, etc.).
2. **Federal Working Language**: Introduction of Amharic as a second language in non-Amharic speaking regions.
3. **English Language**: Taught as an academic subject from Grade 1 to prepare learners for transitional bilingualism.
4. **Mathematics**: Arithmetic, geometric visualization, mental computation, and foundational statistics.
5. **Environmental Science (Grades 1–6)**: Local ecology, highland and lowland geography, water conservation, and soil protection.
6. **Performing & Visual Arts**: Traditional Ethiopian musicology, painting, cultural crafts, and folklore.
7. **Physical & Health Education**: Motor skills development, community hygiene, and nutritional awareness.
8. **Moral & Citizenship Education**: Mutual tolerance, democratic principles, national unity, constitutional awareness, and ethics.

#### Tier 2: Middle School Education (Grades 7–8)
Middle school serves as a bridging tier, fostering scientific inquiry and career orientation:
1. **English Language**: Expanded to the primary medium of instruction for scientific and technical disciplines.
2. **General Science**: Integrated physics, chemistry, biology, and laboratory experiments.
3. **Social Studies**: Horn of Africa history, African geography, and environmental stewardship.
4. **Career & Technical Education (CTE)**: Hands-on pre-vocational modules in electrical wiring, woodcraft, urban agriculture, bookkeeping, and textile arts.
5. **Information & Communication Technology (ICT)**: Computer fundamentals, office productivity software, and internet safety.
6. **Citizenship Education**: The rule of law, human rights, civic participation, and anti-corruption awareness.

#### Tier 3: General Secondary Education (Grades 9–10)
In Grades 9 and 10, all students pursue a rigorous core scientific and humanist curriculum taught exclusively in English:
- *Natural Sciences*: Discrete subjects in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology with mandatory laboratory practicals.
- *Mathematics*: Euclidean geometry, polynomial algebra, trigonometry, and function analysis.
- *Humanities & Social Sciences*: Ethiopian and World History, Human and Physical Geography, Economics.
- *Information Technology*: Algorithmic thinking, flowcharting, and introductory Python programming.

#### Tier 4: Senior Secondary Specialized Streams (Grades 11–12)
At the conclusion of Grade 10, students select one of two specialized pathways based on academic performance and aptitude:

##### Track A: Natural Science Stream
- Advanced Physics (Mechanics, Electromagnetism, Modern Physics)
- Advanced Chemistry (Inorganic, Organic Reaction Mechanisms, Thermochemistry)
- Advanced Biology (Genetics, Molecular Biology, Human Physiology, Botany)
- Technical Mathematics (Calculus, Vectors, Probability Distributions, Complex Numbers)
- English Language & Academic Writing
- Information Technology (Database Management, Networking)

##### Track B: Social Science Stream
- History (African Decolonization, Ethiopian State Formation, Global Geopolitics)
- Geography (Cartography, Climatology, Geographic Information Systems - GIS)
- Economics (Microeconomics, Macroeconomic Theory, Ethiopian Economic Policies)
- General Mathematics (Applied Statistics, Financial Mathematics, Linear Programming)
- English Language & Literature
- Information Technology & Digital Communications

### 4. Integration of Indigenous Knowledge & National Values
The curriculum integrates documented Ethiopian indigenous knowledge systems, such as:
- Indigenous water and moisture harvesting systems (e.g., Konso terracing systems).
- Traditional architectural conservation (e.g., Lalibela rock-hewn stonemasonry and Axum stelae construction engineering).
- Traditional indigenous agronomy (e.g., Enset propagation, highland teff harvesting, and wild forest coffee conservation).
- Indigenous dispute resolution mechanisms (e.g., Gadaa system, Shimagelle, and regional customary councils).
