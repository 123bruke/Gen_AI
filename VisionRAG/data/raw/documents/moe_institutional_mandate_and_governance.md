# Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia: Ministry of Education (MOE)
## Institutional Mandate, Legal Powers & Governance Architecture

### 1. Executive Summary & Statutory Authority
The **Ministry of Education (MOE)** of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia (FDRE) (Amharic: የኢትዮጵያ ፌዴራላዊ ዴሞክራሲያዊ ሪፐብሊክ የትምህርት ሚኒስቴር) is the principal federal executive organ responsible for the formulation, regulation, coordination, and oversight of national educational policies, strategies, and statutory frameworks across all tiers of the Ethiopian educational ecosystem.

The contemporary operational, regulatory, and policy mandate of the Ministry is governed by:
- **General Education Proclamation No. 1368/2025**: Codifies national standards for pre-primary, primary, middle, and secondary education, governance decentralization, inclusive education, and community participation.
- **Regulation No. 561/2025 (Ethiopian Qualifications Framework for Education and Training)**: Establishes the national qualifications architecture, competency-based learning outcomes, and parity of academic and vocational qualifications.
- **Higher Education Proclamation No. 1152/2019**: Directs the governance, public accountability, academic freedom, research missions, and administrative autonomy of higher education institutions across the federation.

### 2. Institutional Headquarters & Official Channels
- **Seat of Governance**: Arada Sub-City, King George VI Avenue, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.
- **Official Web Portal**: `https://moe.gov.et/`
- **Jurisdictional Scope**: Federal oversight of general education, teacher development, educational quality inspection, higher education institutions, digital education infrastructure, and national assessment systems across all 12 regional states and 2 chartered city administrations.

### 3. Core Vision, Mission & Strategic Core Values
- **National Vision**: Sustainably building an equitable, inclusive, and high-quality education and training system that guarantees lifelong learning for all Ethiopian citizens, producing a globally competitive, ethically grounded, and innovative workforce driving socioeconomic transformation.
- **Strategic Mission**: Ensuring access to quality and standard education across the nation through robust institutional implementation capacity, continuous curriculum development, systematic teacher and educational leadership enhancement, modern digital infrastructure, and transparent public accountability.
- **Guiding System Values**:
  1. *Equity and Inclusiveness*: Eradicating geographic, socioeconomic, and gender disparities in educational attainment.
  2. *Quality and Academic Rigor*: Institutionalizing high academic standards, continuous assessment, and evidence-based pedagogy.
  3. *Accountability and Good Governance*: Transparent management of public educational resources and decentralized decision-making.
  4. *Patriotic and Moral Grounding*: Cultivating active citizenship, social harmony, critical inquiry, and respect for diversity.

### 4. Organizational Architecture & Executive Portfolios
The Ministry is structured into specialized executive domains directed by State Ministers and Executive Directors under the leadership of the Federal Minister of Education:

#### A. General Education Sector
- **Curriculum Development Executive**: Oversees syllabi, textbook publishing, learning standards, and competency frameworks for Mother Tongue languages, English, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences, Mathematics, and Career & Technical Education.
- **Teachers and Educational Leaders Development Desk**: Regulates pre-service teacher preparation at Colleges of Teacher Education (CTEs), continuous professional development (CPD), and national licensing frameworks.
- **Education Programs & Quality Improvement Desk**: Administers special initiatives, STEM acceleration, inclusive learning, and secondary education expansion.
- **Pastoralist & Special Needs Education Desk**: Implements targeted interventions, mobile school paradigms for pastoralist populations (Afar, Somali, Borena, South Omo), and assistive infrastructure for students with disabilities.
- **General Education Inspection Desk**: Conducts systemic quality audits and accreditation across general education institutions against national standards.

#### B. Higher Education Sector
- **Higher Education Academic Affairs Executive**: Coordinates undergraduate and postgraduate academic programs, curriculum harmonization, and academic calendars across all 47+ public universities.
- **University Governance & Institutional Autonomy Desk**: Guides the transition of designated public universities (pioneered by Addis Ababa University) toward autonomous governance boards.
- **Research, Ethics & Community Engagement Desk**: Sets national priorities for university-led research, ethical compliance, indigenous knowledge research, and university-industry linkages.
- **Institutional Linkage & Technology Transfer Desk**: Facilitates incubation centers, patent registrations, and technology dissemination between academia and national industrial corridors.

#### C. ICT & Digital Education Sector
- **SchoolNet ICT Desk**: Coordinates satellite, fiber-optic, and microwave broadband connectivity connecting secondary schools and educational bureaus.
- **Educational Multimedia Program Development Desk**: Produces digital curricula, video lectures, and interactive e-learning modules.
- **Educational Media Studio & Broadcast Desk**: Manages national educational television and radio channels for remote and supplementary learning.
- **Network Technical & Operations Desk**: Manages cloud repositories, student information management systems (EMIS), and central data centers.

#### D. Governance, Infrastructure & Planning
- **Planning, Budget & Resource Mobilization Desk**: Formulates multi-year sector plans, capital expenditure allocations, and donor-financed programs (e.g., GEQIP-E, World Bank, UNESCO).
- **Scholarship & International Cooperation Desk**: Administers bilateral educational agreements, student exchange protocols, and foreign scholarship allocations.

### 5. Educational Federalism & Decentralized Administration
Under Ethiopia's decentralized constitutional framework, education delivery operates through three synergistic administrative tiers:
1. **Federal Ministry of Education (MOE)**: Sets national education policies, standards, overarching curriculum frameworks, higher education management, national examinations, and statutory licensing.
2. **Regional Education Bureaus (REBs)**: Responsible for administering regional schools, adapting primary curriculum into local mother tongues, recruiting and deploying primary and secondary teachers, and administering regional primary examinations (Grade 6 and Grade 8).
3. **Woreda (District) Education Offices & Kebele Boards**: Manage local school clusters, day-to-day operations, community mobilization, school feeding logistics, and parent-teacher-student associations (PTSAs).
